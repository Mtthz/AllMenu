# AllMenu

One menu with everything in your game: creatures, blocks, items, structures and dimensions from **Minecraft and all your installed mods**, plus a seed map, an AI helper and your own structures. For **Minecraft 1.20.1** (Forge).

> ⚠️ AllMenu is new and may contain bugs or conflicts with other mods.
> Found one? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) so it can be fixed.

This repository is for **bug reports, crash reports and release notes**.
Downloads are coming soon on CurseForge.

---

## What's inside

- **The menu (O key or `/menu`)**: tabs for Creatures, Blocks, Items, Structures and Dimensions, with everything grouped by mod. One search box works in every tab, and the menu remembers where you were.
- **Creatures**: health, damage, where they spawn (biomes and structures), what they can drop, a 3D preview and whether you have already killed one.
- **Items and blocks**: stats, enchantments, how to get them (crafting, furnace, smithing, stonecutter and recipes from other mods), the recipe grid with clickable ingredients, a 3D preview and the matching Minecraft Wiki article.
- **Seed map (M key)**: a Chunkbase-style map of your world's seed with biomes, structures and slime chunks. Save places with a name and a color: they show up as a beacon beam in the world.
- **Ask the AI (I key)**: ask anything about Minecraft. It uses Google Gemini with your own free API key, Minecraft Wiki articles and what it knows about your game (mods, where you are, your saved places).
- **My structures (B key)**: save any area with the selector stick (`/build`), add your own `.nbt` and `.schem` files, preview them as a ghost, rotate and build them. You can also let them generate naturally in the Overworld, the Nether and The End.
- **In Creative or with cheats on**: spawn creatures, give yourself items and blocks (Shift = full stack), locate and build structures, and teleport to dimensions and places.
- Available in **English** and **Portuguese (Brazil)**.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.20.1**
- Forge **47** or newer
- For multiplayer, install AllMenu on the server too.

## Reporting a bug

1. Check if it was already reported in [Issues](../../issues).
2. [Open a new issue](../../issues/new/choose) using the **Bug report** or **Crash report** form.
3. For crashes, attach the file from `crash-reports` and your `logs/latest.log`.
